# Restaurant List photos

All three card photos are the original images supplied by the student for the October 2 wireframe. The Midyeci Riza and La Unica originals recovered on October 10 have byte hashes matching the images uploaded to Figma. They are bundled locally, so the demo needs no network connection to display them.
